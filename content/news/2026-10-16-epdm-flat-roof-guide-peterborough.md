EPDM is a single-sheet synthetic rubber membrane, laid in one piece over a flat roof deck. It has become the go-to choice for garages, extensions and dormers because it is durable, quick to fit and has very few joints for water to find.

## How much does an EPDM roof cost?

As a 2026 guide, EPDM typically costs **£90 – £140 per m²** supplied and fitted. That usually means:

- **Single garage or small extension roof (up to about 15 m²):** £1,400 – £2,900
- **Larger flat roof (25 – 40 m²):** £2,500 – £5,000

The price depends on whether the deck underneath needs replacing, how much insulation is required, and access.

## How long does it last?

A correctly installed EPDM roof can last **30 to 50 years**. Traditional felt roofs often need attention after 10 to 20 years, which is why many homeowners choose EPDM when the old felt fails.

## Is EPDM right for my roof?

EPDM works well for:

- Garages and outbuildings
- Single-storey kitchen and living-room extensions
- Dormers and flat sections of larger roofs

The key is preparation. A sound, dry deck, correct falls so water drains, and properly finished edges and upstands make the difference between a roof that lasts 10 years and one that lasts 40.
