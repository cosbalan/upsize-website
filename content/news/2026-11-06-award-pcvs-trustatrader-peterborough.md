Peterborough roofing contractor Upsize Property Renovation Ltd has had a busy autumn. ## Quality Business Awards 2026: Best Roofing, Peterborough

The [Quality Business Awards](https://qualitybusinessawards.co.uk/2026/the-best-roofing-in-peterborough/upsize-property-renovation-ltd) recognise local businesses based on their customer review ratings. Upsize was named the 2026 winner for **Best Roofing in Peterborough**.

## Member of Peterborough Council for Voluntary Service

In November 2026 Upsize became a **member of [PCVS](https://www.pcvs.co.uk/)**, the support organisation for charities, community groups and voluntary organisations in Peterborough.

We joined to work properly alongside the local voluntary sector. Through PCVS's volunteering platform, [Go-ViP](https://www.go-vip.co.uk/o/Upsize-Property-Renovation-Ltd/), we offer voluntary and community organisations:

- **free roof and building checks** for community premises;
- **volunteering days** on community buildings;
- **talks for older people's groups** on spotting roof problems early and avoiding rogue traders.

PCVS supports charities and community groups. It does not deliver or take referrals for our Weekend Roof Clinic, so residents and support workers should contact us directly.

## How to check any roofer before you book

Whoever you choose, a few minutes of checks can save you from a bad job:

| Check | Where to look |
| --- | --- |
| Is the company real? | Search the name on Companies House |
| Are the reviews independent? | Google, TrustATrader and other verified review sites, not just the roofer's own website |
| Are they insured? | Ask for public liability insurance details in writing |
| Is the price fixed? | Get a written quote that lists the work, the materials and the guarantee |
| Do they push you to decide today? | A good roofer never asks for cash up front or pressures you on the doorstep |
