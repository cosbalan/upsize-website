How much does a new roof cost in Peterborough?

As a guide, a full re-roof with concrete tiles costs around £5,200–£8,600 for a terraced house, £7,500–£12,100 for a 3-bed semi and £9,200–£14,900 for a detached house, including scaffolding and waste removal. We give you a fixed, written price after a free roof check.

How much does a roof repair cost?

Small repairs, such as replacing slipped or cracked tiles, usually cost £170–£460. Re-bedding a full ridge is typically £500–£1,400. We photograph the problem first, so you see exactly what needs fixing before you agree a price.

Should I repair or replace my roof?

If the timbers and most of the tiles are sound, a repair is usually better value. Replacement makes sense when repairs would cost more than about a quarter of a new roof, or when the covering has reached the end of its life. We always advise repair first where it genuinely works.

What are the signs I need a new roof?

Common signs are damp patches on ceilings, daylight showing in the loft, a sagging roof line, lots of slipped or cracked tiles, crumbling ridge mortar and heavy moss. One sign on its own often means a repair; several together usually point to a re-roof.

How long does it take to replace a roof?

Most houses take 3–8 working days, depending on size and access. A terraced house is often 3–5 days and a 3-bed semi 5–8 days. Scaffolding goes up first, and the roof is kept watertight at the end of every day.

How much does an EPDM rubber flat roof cost and how long does it last?

EPDM rubber roofing typically costs £90–£140 per m² fitted, so a garage or small extension roof is usually £1,400–£2,900. A correctly installed EPDM roof can last 30–50 years, much longer than traditional felt.

Do I need planning permission or building regulations to replace my roof?

Planning permission is not normally needed to replace a house roof like-for-like. Building regulations approval is usually needed if you re-roof more than a quarter of the roof area or change the insulation. We will tell you what applies to your roof before work starts.

Does home insurance cover roof leaks and storm damage?

Most home insurance covers sudden storm damage but not wear and tear or gradual leaks. Check your policy. We can provide dated photos and a written report of the damage to support your claim.

Do you offer free roof inspections in Peterborough?

Yes. We offer free roof checks across Peterborough and up to 40 miles around, with photos of what we find and a written quote. There is no obligation to go ahead.

Do you guarantee your roofing work?

Yes. Every roofing job comes with a 10-year written guarantee. It is written up and handed to you on the spot when the job is signed off.

Do you do commercial roofing?

Yes. We repair and replace flat and pitched roofs on commercial units, offices and managed property, including EPDM flat roofing, and can plan the work around your opening hours.

Do you install wet rooms and level-access showers?

Yes. We install wet rooms and level-access showers for social housing adaptations, commercial property and private homes, including non-slip flooring, shower seats and grab rails.

Do you fit kitchens and bathrooms for homeowners and businesses?

Yes. We supply and fit kitchens, bathrooms and wet rooms for homeowners, landlords and commercial clients, with experience installing Howdens and Symphony kitchens.

Most kitchen or bathroom replacements take around 5 working days. One Upsize team handles every trade, from strip-out to decoration, so there are no gaps waiting for other trades.

How long does a social housing kitchen or bathroom replacement take?

Five working days per property. One Upsize team covers every trade, from strip-out to decoration, in both void and occupied homes.
