Roofing in March

Homes and roofs in March

March is a Fenland market town on the old course of the River Nene, with Victorian and Edwardian terraces from its railway days, the famous angel roof of St Wendreda’s church, and large newer estates.

What we usually find on roofs here

The older terraces need slate and chimney work; the estates mostly need ridge, verge and flat roof repairs. Open fenland winds add storm damage most winters.

Local tip from our roofers

In March’s Victorian terraces, chimney stacks are often shared with the house next door. If yours needs repointing or new flashings, it is worth talking to your neighbour: one scaffold can do both sides and save you both money.

Do you repair slate roofs on older terraces in March?

Yes. We replace slipped and broken slates, renew nail fixings with clips where needed, and repoint chimneys and replace lead flashings.

How much does a roof repair cost in March?

After a storm, refixing slipped tiles on a March roof usually costs £170–£460, and refixing a full ridge with a dry-fix system £500–£1,400. We give you a fixed, written price and photos for your insurer after a free drone check.

Do I need permission for roof work in March?

March town centre is a conservation area. Fenland District Council is the planning authority. For March roofs we check what applies before starting, and arrange Building Control sign-off where a re-roof needs it.
