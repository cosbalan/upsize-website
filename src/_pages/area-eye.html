Homes and roofs in Eye

Eye sits on the fen edge north-east of Peterborough, with older brick homes along the main road, plenty of bungalows and newer estates on the edges of the village and Eye Green.

What we usually find on roofs here

Bungalow roofs here are low and exposed, so wind-lifted verges, slipped tiles and blocked gutters are the common call-outs.

Local tip from our roofers

If you have a bungalow in Eye, your roof is easier to inspect but takes the wind straight off the fen. Look out for verge tiles lifting at the gable ends and gutters pulling away at the corners; both are quick fixes if caught early.
