What we usually find on roofs here

Local tip from our roofers

Many Chatteris homes are bungalows with low-pitched roofs, which shed water more slowly and suit the right tile and underlay for the pitch. If you are re-roofing, check the new tiles are rated for your roof pitch; we always do.
