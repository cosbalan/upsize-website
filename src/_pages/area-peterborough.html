Roofing in Peterborough

Homes and roofs in Peterborough

Peterborough has every kind of roof. Victorian and Edwardian terraces around Millfield, New England and Park Road often still have their original Welsh slate. The new-town estates of the 1970s and 80s in Bretton, Orton and Werrington are mostly concrete interlocking tiles, and Hampton and Paston Reserve add thousands of newer homes with tiled roofs, dormers and flat-roofed garages.

What we usually find on roofs here

On the older terraces we mostly see slipped slates, failing nail fixings and worn lead in the valleys and back additions. On the estates it is crumbling ridge mortar, wind-lifted verges and tired felt flat roofs on garages and extensions.

Local tip from our roofers

If you live on one of the 1970s or 80s estates in Bretton, the Ortons or Werrington, many original roofs are now 40–50 years old. The tiles often have plenty of life left, but the felt underneath and the ridge mortar may not, so a targeted repair can buy years before a full re-roof. In the older terraces of Millfield and New England, check the rear valleys and back-addition roofs first: that is where most leaks start.
