Homes and roofs in Wansford

Wansford is a limestone village either side of the old River Nene bridge, with stone cottages and houses, many with stone slate or clay tile roofs, close to the A1.

What we usually find on roofs here

We mostly see stone slate and tile repairs, chimney repointing and lead work at abutments on the older properties.

Local tip from our roofers

Wansford’s riverside cottages sit low by the Nene and can stay damp, so moss builds up quickly on north-facing slopes. Moss holds water against tiles and lifts them in a frost. Gentle moss removal and keeping gutters clear makes a big difference here.
