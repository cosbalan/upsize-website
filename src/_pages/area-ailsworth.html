Homes and roofs in Ailsworth

Ailsworth runs into Castor along the A47, with a mix of traditional limestone houses and later brick homes. Older roofs are often clay tile or stone slate; newer ones concrete tile.

What we usually find on roofs here

We see worn ridge and hip mortar, lifted clay tiles and tired lead around chimneys on the older homes, and garage and extension flat roofs on the newer ones.

Local tip from our roofers

Many Ailsworth homes back onto open fields along the A47, so the rear slopes take the worst of the weather. If you see clay tile fragments on the patio or in the gutters, ask for a check of the hips and ridge, where loose tiles usually start.
