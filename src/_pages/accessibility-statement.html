1. What we have done

  • Built to work with screen readers and keyboard-only navigation
  • Descriptive text for all meaningful images
  • Strong colour contrast and readable text sizes
  • Pages that resize for phones and tablets without sideways scrolling
  • Animations that stop if your device is set to reduce motion

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA.

2. Need help or another format?

If you have difficulty using any part of this website, contact us on 0800 193 9030 or at alex@upsizeproperty.com, and we will provide the information in another way, such as by phone, large print or email.
