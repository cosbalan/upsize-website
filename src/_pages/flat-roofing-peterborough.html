Expert guide

Why flat roofs fail, and how we build them to last

Most flat roof problems come from three things: water lying on the roof, poor detailing at the edges and outlets, and materials past their life. Traditional felt roofs often last only 10–20 years. A correctly installed EPDM rubber roof can last 30–50 years.

Falls matter more than anything

The British Standard for flat roofs, BS 6229:2018, says roofs should be designed with a 1:40 fall so that a minimum of 1:80 is achieved on the finished roof. A “flat” roof that holds puddles after rain will fail early. We check the deck and correct the falls before laying the membrane.

Insulation and Building Regulations

Replacing a flat roof is often more than a new covering. When a roof is both re-decked and re-covered, building control guidance says the insulation must be upgraded to current standards, under Part L of the Building Regulations. We include this in our price where it applies, so there are no surprises.

Why EPDM?

  • One seamless sheet across most roofs, so there are fewer joints to fail
  • Flexible in hot and cold weather, and resistant to UV
  • No naked flames used in installation, so it is safer for occupied homes
  • Low maintenance and easy to repair if damaged

FAQs

Flat roofing: your questions answered

How much does a flat roof cost in Peterborough?

EPDM rubber roofing typically costs £80–£120 per m² fitted. A garage or small extension roof is usually £1,200–£2,500, and a larger single-storey extension £1,800–£3,500, depending on the deck, insulation and edges.

How long does an EPDM flat roof last?

A correctly installed EPDM roof can last 30–50 years, much longer than traditional felt, which often lasts 10–20 years.

Is EPDM better than felt?

For most domestic roofs, yes. EPDM is a single seamless sheet, stays flexible in all weathers, resists UV and is installed without naked flames. Felt is cheaper upfront but usually needs replacing much sooner.

Why does water pond on my flat roof?

Usually because the roof has too little fall or the deck has sagged. BS 6229 recommends designing for a 1:40 fall so that at least 1:80 is achieved. We correct the falls when replacing the roof.

Do I need building regulations approval to replace a flat roof?

Often, yes, if you are replacing the deck as well as the covering, because the insulation then needs upgrading under Part L. A like-for-like re-cover of a small area may not need approval. We tell you what applies.

Can a flat roof be installed in winter?

Yes, in dry conditions. EPDM adhesives need dry surfaces and suitable temperatures, so we plan around the weather and protect the roof between stages.

Can you repair my flat roof instead of replacing it?

If the damage is local and the deck is sound, yes. If the felt is blistered and cracked across the roof, or the deck is soft, replacement is better value.
