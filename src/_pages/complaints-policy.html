Complaints Procedure

If you are unhappy with our work or service, please tell us so we can put it right. We treat every complaint seriously and use it to improve.

1. How to complain

Contact us by email at alex@upsizeproperty.com, by phone on 0800 193 9030, or by post to our registered office: 4 Blenheim Court, Suite 595, Peterborough, PE1 2DU. Please include your name, address, the job and what has gone wrong.

If we are working for your landlord or housing provider, you can also complain through them, and we will work with them to resolve it.

2. What happens next

StageWhat we doTimescale
AcknowledgementWe confirm we have received your complaint and who is dealing with it.Within 2 working days
Stage 1: investigationWe look into it, visit if needed and give you a full written response.Within 10 working days
Stage 2: Director reviewIf you are not satisfied, the Director reviews the complaint and our response.Within 10 working days of your request

If we need longer, we will tell you why and when to expect a response. Urgent safety issues, such as leaks or electrical faults, are attended to as a priority.

3. If you are still not satisfied

Social housing residents can refer the complaint to their landlord’s complaints process and then to the Housing Ombudsman. Homeowners can seek independent advice from Citizens Advice.

4. Learning from complaints

All complaints are logged and reviewed by the Director as part of our quality management review.
