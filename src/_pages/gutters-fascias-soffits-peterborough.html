Warning signs

When your roofline needs attention

Small roofline problems are cheap to fix early. Left alone, overflowing water soaks brickwork, rots roof timbers and causes damp inside.

Get my free roofing quote
  • Water spilling over the gutter edge when it rains
  • Green or dark streaks down the brickwork
  • Sagging gutters or joints dripping
  • Flaking paint, soft or rotten timber fascias
  • Damp patches on upstairs ceilings near the eaves
  • Birds or pests getting in behind the soffits

FAQs

Gutters, fascias & soffits: your questions answered

Can't see your question? Call 0800 193 9030 or send us a message.

How much do new fascias, soffits and gutters cost?

As a guide, replacing the gutters and downpipes on a 3-bed semi usually costs £400–£900. Replacing fascias, soffits and gutters together is typically £1,800–£3,500 for a semi, depending on the length of the eaves and access. We give a fixed, written price after a free check.

Why are my gutters overflowing?

The usual causes are blockages from leaves, moss and silt, sagging brackets that let water pool, joints that have pulled apart, or gutters that are too small for the roof area. We find the cause before recommending anything.

How long do uPVC fascias, soffits and gutters last?

Good-quality uPVC fascias, soffits and gutters can last 20–30 years or more. Brackets and seals usually need attention sooner, so a check every few years helps them last.

Can you replace fascias and gutters at the same time as roof work?

Yes, and it usually saves money. If scaffolding is already up for roof repairs or a re-roof, replacing fascias, soffits and gutters at the same time avoids paying for access twice.

Do you clear and repair gutters as well as replace them?

Yes. We clear blocked gutters and downpipes, re-align sagging runs, replace brackets and reseal leaking joints. Our free drone roof check shows blocked or damaged gutters before you book anything.

Do I need scaffolding for gutter and fascia work?

For safe work at height, most fascia replacements need scaffolding or a tower. Smaller gutter repairs can sometimes be done with other safe access. We use our own scaffolding for most domestic jobs, so you are not left waiting.
