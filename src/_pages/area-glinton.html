What we usually find on roofs here

Local tip from our roofers

Glinton’s older stone houses often have clay tiles and lead-lined valleys that are decades old. If a ceiling stain appears near a chimney or where two roof slopes meet, it is usually the lead, not the tiles, and a valley or flashing repair is far cheaper than re-roofing.
