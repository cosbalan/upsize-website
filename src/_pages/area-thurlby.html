Homes and roofs in Thurlby

Thurlby is a village just south of Bourne on the fen edge, with older brick and stone cottages and newer homes, many with concrete or clay tile roofs.

What we usually find on roofs here

Wind across the fen edge loosens ridges, verges and tiles, and older chimneys often need repointing and new flashings.

Local tip from our roofers

Thurlby homes on the fen side of the village feel the wind most. If you have an older roof with mortar-bedded ridges, cracks along the ridge line are a sign to act before tiles start to move.
