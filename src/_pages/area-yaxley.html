Roofing in Yaxley

Homes and roofs in Yaxley

Yaxley is a large village just south of Peterborough. Around the older centre near St Peter’s Church there are brick and stone cottages, but most homes are on estates and bungalow roads built from the 1960s to the 1980s, many with concrete tile roofs.

What we usually find on roofs here

Many of those concrete tile roofs are now 40–60 years old. We often find mortar ridges and verges cracking, old bitumen felt perished under the tiles, and flat-roofed garages and dormers coming to the end of their life.

Local tip from our roofers

If your Yaxley roof has original concrete tiles and the felt underneath is crumbling, a repair can buy time, but it is worth getting the whole roof checked. Our drone survey shows you the condition of every slope, so you can decide between repairing now and planning a re-roof.

Local roofing notes

Ageing estate roofs and bungalows

Yaxley has a lot of bungalows, and bungalow roofs are large for the size of the house. When the ridges, valleys and felt fail at the same time, we price repairs and a full re-roof side by side so you can compare.

Yaxley is in Huntingdonshire. Like-for-like roof repairs and replacements do not normally need planning permission, but a re-roof usually needs Building Regulations sign-off, which we arrange for you.

How much does a roof repair cost in Yaxley?

Replacing a few slipped tiles usually costs £170–£460, and lead flashing around a chimney is typically £400–£1,050. Ridge re-bedding and flat roof replacement are priced after a free drone check. You get a fixed, written price before we start.

Should I repair or replace my old concrete tile roof?

If the tiles are sound and the problem is local, a repair makes sense. If the felt underneath has perished across the roof, or several slopes are leaking, a re-roof is often better value. We show you the drone photos and price both.

Can you replace the flat roof on my garage or dormer?

Yes. We replace old felt flat roofs with EPDM rubber or GRP fibreglass, usually in one or two days, with a new deck where the old one has rotted.
