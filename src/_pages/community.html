Peterborough Weekend Roof Clinic: supporting our local community

Free roof inspections, essential safety advice and minor remedial fixes for elderly homeowners, vulnerable residents and local families struggling with maintenance costs across Peterborough, delivered at weekends by our co-directors, Alexandru Balan and Laurentiu Dobinda.

Why we're doing it

Protecting vulnerable residents from winter weather damage

With winter bringing severe rain and freezing temperatures, unaddressed roof leaks, loose tiles and blocked guttering can quickly lead to severe damp, heat loss and thousands of pounds in emergency repair costs.

The initiative offers clear property assessments alongside free immediate minor repairs where feasible, or discounted restoration work for those requiring extensive support. Inspections and minor remedial works run on weekends throughout the autumn and winter months.

Upsize is a paying member of Peterborough Council for Voluntary Service (PCVS), the umbrella body for local charities and community groups. Charities, community hubs and support workers can refer residents to the Weekend Roof Clinic through us or through PCVS.

"In our day-to-day work across social housing, we constantly see the stress that sudden home repair issues place on vulnerable people. We wanted to do more than just standard business. By dedicating our weekends, we can personally step in, inspect roofs, handle minor fixes on the spot, and give local residents complete peace of mind without financial strain."

Alexandru Balan, Director, Upsize Property Renovation Ltd
The Weekend Roof Clinic

What we offer and who can apply

What we offer

  • Free visual roof and chimney stack inspections
  • Complimentary minor remedial fixes, such as clearing blocked gutters, resetting loose tiles and minor sealant fixes
  • Honest, jargon-free repair advice and a written assessment
  • Subsidised discounts on major structural works for verified cases in need

Who is eligible

  • Pensioners
  • Low-income households
  • Disabled residents
  • Community members in financial hardship

Family members, carers and neighbours can nominate someone on their behalf.
