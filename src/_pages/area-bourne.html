Homes and roofs in Bourne

Bourne is a market town on the edge of the fens, with older stone and brick properties around the centre and Bourne Abbey, and large newer estates such as Elsea Park.

What we usually find on roofs here

Newer homes mostly need dry ridge and verge repairs, valley leaks and dormer or garage flat roofs; older town-centre homes need slate, lead and chimney work.

Local tip from our roofers

On newer Bourne estates such as Elsea Park, roofs are generally sound but small installation faults show up after a few winters: loose dry ridge caps, lifted verge units and leaking dormer flashings. These are quick, low-cost fixes that are well worth doing early.
