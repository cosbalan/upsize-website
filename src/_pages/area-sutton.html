Homes and roofs in Sutton

Sutton is a small Nene Valley village just west of Castor, with a handful of traditional stone properties and newer homes close to the A47.

What we usually find on roofs here

Exposed roofs above the Nene Valley take the wind, so we mostly see slipped tiles, loose ridges and worn flashings.

Local tip from our roofers

Sutton’s handful of older stone properties and newer homes sit high above the Nene Valley, so ridge tiles and verges carry most of the wind load. Dry-fix ridge and verge systems are a good long-term fix here, because there is no mortar to wash out.
