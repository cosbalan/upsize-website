Roofing in Stamford

Homes and roofs in Stamford

Stamford is one of England’s finest stone towns and in 1967 became the country’s first designated conservation area. Its limestone buildings are roofed in Collyweston stone slate, Welsh slate and clay tile, with newer estates on the edges of town.

What we usually find on roofs here

Stone slate roofs need specialist repairs, and parapet gutters, valleys and chimneys on period buildings need careful lead work.

Local tip from our roofers

If your Stamford home is listed or in the conservation area, get advice before any roof work beyond like-for-like repairs. Matching Collyweston or Welsh slate and lead details keeps the roof in character and avoids problems with the council later.
