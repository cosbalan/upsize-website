Homes and roofs in Kettering

Kettering grew on the boot and shoe trade, so it has many Victorian brick terraces with slate roofs, plus post-war estates and large newer developments.

What we usually find on roofs here

Terraces need slate, chimney and lead work; estates need ridge, verge and flat roof repairs and re-roofs.

Local tip from our roofers

Kettering’s Victorian terraces often have slate roofs on their second or third life. If slates are slipping regularly, the nails have rusted through; re-slating with new fixings may be better value than repeated repairs.
