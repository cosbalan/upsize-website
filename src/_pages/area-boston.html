How much does a roof repair cost in Boston?

After a storm, refixing slipped tiles on a Boston roof usually costs £170–£460, and refixing a full ridge with a dry-fix system £500–£1,400. We give you a fixed, written price and photos for your insurer after a free drone check.

Do I need permission for roof work in Boston?

Boston has a conservation area with many listed buildings. Boston Borough Council is the planning authority. For Boston roofs we check what applies before starting, and arrange Building Control sign-off where a re-roof needs it.
