How a re-roof works

Your new roof, step by step

A typical house re-roof takes 3–8 working days. This is what happens, and what you'll see each day.

  1. Free drone survey and fixed price

    We photograph every slope, ridge and chimney, then give you a fixed, written price. Book a free drone roof survey.

  2. Scaffold and protection

    Scaffolding goes up with edge protection. We protect the garden, drive and loft, and agree where the skip goes.

  3. Strip and check the timbers

    Old tiles, battens and felt come off. We check rafters for rot or sag and show you photos before any extra work.

  4. Membrane, battens and ventilation

    Breathable membrane, new treated battens and roof ventilation, so the loft stays dry and condensation-free.

  5. Tiles, dry-fix ridge and verges

    New tiles or slates, mechanically fixed ridges and verges to BS 5534, and new lead or flashings where needed.

  6. Watertight every night

    The roof is covered and watertight at the end of every working day, whatever the weather forecast.

  7. Clean-up and handover

    Gutters cleared, waste removed, drone photos of the finished roof, and your written workmanship guarantee.

Full details, materials and prices are on our roof replacement in Peterborough page. For a leak, slipped tiles or a loose ridge, see roof repairs in Peterborough.

Roofing FAQs

Roofing questions, answered

Can't see your question? Call 0800 193 9030 or send us a message.

Is the drone roof check really free?

Yes. A drone roof survey usually costs around £250, but ours is free with every roofing enquiry. We fly it on the spot and show you the close-up photos there and then, with no obligation. If you need a detailed written photo report, for example for an insurer or a house sale, we can provide one; there may be a small charge, which we always tell you about first.

What guarantee do I get on my roof?

Every roofing job comes with our 10-year written workmanship guarantee, handed to you when the job is signed off. Materials also carry manufacturer guarantees of up to 20–25 years, depending on the products, and an insurance-backed guarantee is available for extra peace of mind (T&Cs apply).

Are you vetted on TrustATrader?

Yes. Upsize Property Renovation Ltd is a verified TrustATrader member. TrustATrader checks members before listing them, and you can read and leave reviews on our TrustATrader profile, alongside our 25+ Google reviews.

Do you offer an insurance-backed guarantee?

Yes, as an option. We work with leading UK insurance-backed guarantee providers. An insurance-backed guarantee protects your guarantee for its full term, even if the company that did the work stops trading. Terms and conditions apply, and we explain the cost before you decide.

How do I pay for my roof?

A deposit secures your start date and the materials, which we buy direct from our own suppliers. Larger jobs are paid in agreed stages as the work is completed, and everything is set out in a written contract you sign before we start. You can pay by contactless card, cash or bank transfer (BACS), and finance is available on larger projects, subject to status.

How quickly will you get back to me?

We call you back within 15 minutes during opening hours, Monday to Saturday. For roof checks we can often visit the same or next day and give you a fixed price on the spot or the same day.

How soon can you start, and who arranges the scaffolding?

We use our own scaffolding for most domestic jobs, or trusted local suppliers when needed, so we can usually start sooner than roofers who have to wait for a scaffolder. On larger roofs we bring a bigger team so the work finishes faster.

Can you arrange an independent inspection of the finished roof?

Yes. On request, we arrange a competent, independent RICS roof inspection of the finished work, so you have an expert’s report on the quality of the job.

Which roof tiles are best for a house in Peterborough?

For most 1950s–2000s homes, concrete interlocking tiles are the best value and last 50 years or more. Older and village properties often suit clay tiles, and Victorian terraces usually have natural slate. We match what suits your house and any conservation rules, and show you samples first.

What is a dry-fix ridge, and do I need one?

A dry-fix ridge is fixed with mechanical clips and screws instead of relying on mortar alone. BS 5534 requires ridges and verges to be mechanically fixed, so they are far less likely to blow off in a storm. We fit dry-fix systems on every new roof and on ridge repairs where mortar has failed.

Do you remove and dispose of the old roof?

Yes. Stripping the old tiles, battens and felt, the skip and licensed waste disposal are included in our fixed price, and we clear gutters and the site before we leave.

What happens if it rains during a re-roof?

We only strip as much as we can make watertight the same day, and the roof is covered with membrane or sheeting at the end of every working day. If heavy rain or high winds are forecast, we adjust the plan rather than risk your home.

Do you need to go into my loft?

Usually only briefly, to check the timbers, insulation and ventilation and to protect anything stored underneath the work area. We'll ask you to clear a path if needed and tell you in advance.

Will my new roof need ventilation?

Yes. Modern breathable membranes and roof or ridge vents stop condensation building up in the loft, which can rot timbers and damage insulation. We include the right ventilation in every re-roof price.
