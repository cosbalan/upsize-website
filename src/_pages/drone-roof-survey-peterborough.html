What the survey shows

FAQs

Drone roof surveys: your questions answered

Can't see your question? Call 0800 193 9030 or send us a message.

Is the drone roof survey really free?

Yes. Every roofing enquiry gets a free drone roof survey with no obligation. You see the close-up photos on the spot and get a fixed, written price for any work. If you want a separate detailed written report, for example for a house purchase, we tell you the cost before we start.

What can a drone roof survey show?

Slipped, cracked or missing tiles and slates, loose ridge and hip tiles, lifted lead flashings, chimney condition, blocked gutters and valleys, and splits, blisters or ponding on flat roofs. It shows the whole roof in minutes without anyone walking on it.

How long does a drone roof survey take?

Usually under 30 minutes on site for a typical house. We show you the photos straight away and talk you through what we found.

Can you do a flat roof survey with a drone?

Yes. A drone is ideal for flat roofs on extensions, garages and dormers. It shows standing water, blisters, cracks and lifted seams, so we can tell you whether a repair will last or whether an EPDM replacement is better value.

Can I use the drone photos for an insurance claim?

Yes. Dated close-up photos of the damage and of the rest of the roof help your insurer see that the damage is new rather than wear and tear. We can also give an itemised repair price for your claim.

What happens if the weather is bad?

Drones can't fly safely in strong wind or heavy rain. If the weather is poor on the day, we rebook at the first dry slot or, if the roof is leaking, make it safe first.

Do you follow the drone rules?

Yes. We fly in line with the Civil Aviation Authority's Drone and Model Aircraft Code, keep the drone in sight, and only photograph your property.
