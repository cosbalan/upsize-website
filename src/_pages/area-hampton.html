Roofing in Hampton

Homes and roofs in Hampton

Hampton is Peterborough’s newest township, built from the late 1990s onwards south of the city. Most houses have concrete interlocking tiles with dry-fixed ridges and verges, uPVC fascias and long gutter runs.

What we usually find on roofs here

Dry verge caps and ridge clips are the most common Hampton repair. Plastic verge caps crack or blow off in strong winds, and ridge units can loosen when fixings fail. Blocked gutters and leaking joints on long uPVC runs come a close second.

Local tip from our roofers

If a verge cap has blown off, get it replaced quickly. Without it, wind-driven rain gets straight onto the batten ends and the gable wall. If your home is under 10 years old, check your new-home warranty first; our drone photos and written notes can help with a claim.

Local roofing notes

Newer homes, different repairs

Roofs in Hampton are young compared with the rest of Peterborough, so full re-roofs are rare. What we see instead are small failures in the modern dry-fix systems: verge caps, ridge clips, unclipped tiles on exposed edges, and flat roofs over garages, porches and dormers.

Hampton sits within Peterborough City Council. Like-for-like repairs do not need planning permission, but some estates have covenants or management companies, so if you are changing the look of the roof (for example the tile colour), check your deeds first.

How much does a roof repair cost in Hampton?

Replacing a few slipped tiles usually costs £170–£460. Re-fixing or replacing dry verge caps and ridge units is priced after a free drone check, depending on how many metres need doing. You get a fixed, written price before we start.

My verge caps keep blowing off. Can you fix them for good?

Usually, yes. We check whether the caps are the right type for your tiles and whether they were fixed properly, then replace them with a mechanically fixed system so they stay put in high winds.

My house is still under warranty. Should I call you or the builder?

Check your new-home warranty first, because some defects in the first years are the builder’s responsibility. If the problem is not covered, or you need evidence for a claim, our drone photos and written notes can help.
