Do you do commercial roofing?

Yes. We repair and replace flat and pitched roofs on commercial units, offices and managed property, including EPDM flat roofing, and can plan the work around your opening hours.

Do you install wet rooms and level-access showers?

Yes. We install wet rooms and level-access showers for social housing adaptations, commercial property and private homes, including non-slip flooring, shower seats and grab rails.

Are you insured and are your operatives vetted?

Yes. We carry £5m public liability insurance, and every operative is CSCS-carded and DBS-checked.

Which areas do you cover?

Peterborough and up to 40 miles around it, including Stamford, Spalding, Bourne, Market Deeping, Holbeach, Wisbech, March, Huntingdon, St Neots, Oakham, Corby, Kettering and Grantham.
