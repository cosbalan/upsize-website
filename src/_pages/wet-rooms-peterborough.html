What the work involves

From strip-out to a watertight, non-slip room

  1. Strip-out

    The existing bath or tray, tiles and flooring are removed.

  2. Floor & tanking

    The floor is laid to fall towards the drain, then floor and walls are fully waterproofed.

  3. Finishes & fittings

    Non-slip flooring, wall finishes, shower, seat, grab rails, basin and WC.

  4. Test & handover

    Everything is tested and handed over with photos of the finished room.
