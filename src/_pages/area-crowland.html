Roofing in Crowland

Homes and roofs in Crowland

Crowland is a small fen town known for its abbey ruins and the medieval Trinity Bridge, with brick and stone houses, many with clay pantile roofs, and newer homes around the edges.

What we usually find on roofs here

Open fenland means strong winds, so loose pantiles, failing ridges and verges are the most common jobs here.

Local tip from our roofers

Pantile roofs in Crowland move in high winds because each tile overlaps only its neighbours. If you can see daylight between pantiles from the loft, or tiles have shuffled out of line, we re-lay and clip them so they stay put.
