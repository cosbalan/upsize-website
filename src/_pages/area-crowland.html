What should I do if tiles blow off my roof in Crowland?

Stay off the roof and keep clear of the area below. Photograph the damage from the ground, call your insurer if it was storm damage, and call or WhatsApp us for a same-day make-safe where possible.

Why do roofs in Crowland suffer wind damage?

Open fenland gives little shelter, so wind uplift is strong at ridges, verges and eaves. Tiles held only by old mortar are the first to go. We refix them mechanically, as BS 5534 requires.
