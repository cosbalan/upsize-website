Homes and roofs in Holbeach

Holbeach is a fenland market town surrounded by open farmland, with Georgian and Victorian buildings around the centre, many bungalows and newer homes.

What we usually find on roofs here

Open fen winds mean regular storm damage: slipped tiles, loose ridges and verges, and gutters torn loose.

Local tip from our roofers

Around Holbeach, wind off the open farmland lifts loose tiles most winters. Mechanically fixing every tile on exposed edges, as BS 5534 requires, is the difference between a roof that stays put and one that needs repairs every year.
