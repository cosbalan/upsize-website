Health & Safety Policy

Upsize Property Renovation Ltd is committed to protecting the health, safety and welfare of our employees, subcontractors, clients, residents and members of the public affected by our work.

We will comply with the Health and Safety at Work etc. Act 1974, the Construction (Design and Management) Regulations 2015 and all other relevant legislation. We will assess risks and issue risk assessments and method statements (RAMS) for every job, provide suitable training, equipment and supervision, manage asbestos, working at height and other high-risk activities safely, and record and investigate accidents and near misses.

Everyone working for us must follow safe systems of work and report hazards immediately.

Quality Policy

Our aim is to deliver every job right first time, on programme and to the agreed specification.

We achieve this by planning work in detail before starting, using one competent multi-trade team, carrying out quality inspections and snagging before handover, and providing a photo-evidenced handover on every job. We listen to feedback from clients and residents and use it to improve how we work.

Environmental Policy

We aim to reduce the environmental impact of our work. We segregate and recycle waste where possible and use licensed waste carriers, plan routes and deliveries to reduce travel, order materials accurately to reduce waste, and comply with all relevant environmental legislation.

Safeguarding Policy

We often work in occupied homes, including those of children and adults at risk. Everyone working for us must treat residents with respect and must never be alone with a child or vulnerable adult where it can be avoided.

Operatives working in occupied homes are DBS-checked. Any concern about the welfare of a resident must be reported to a director the same day. Where someone is at immediate risk, we will contact the emergency services, and we will share concerns with the relevant local authority safeguarding team and our client.

Modern Slavery Statement

We have zero tolerance of modern slavery and human trafficking in our business and supply chain. We check the right to work of everyone we employ, pay at least the National Minimum or Living Wage, and expect our subcontractors and suppliers to meet the same standards. Anyone can raise a concern in confidence with a director.

Equality & Diversity Policy

We are committed to equality of opportunity and to treating everyone with dignity and respect. We will not discriminate on the grounds of any protected characteristic under the Equality Act 2010, whether in recruitment, employment, choosing subcontractors or providing our services. Harassment and bullying will not be tolerated.

Complaints Procedure

If you are unhappy with our work or service, please tell us so we can put it right. Contact us by email at alex@upsizeproperty.com or by phone on 0800 193 9030.

We will acknowledge your complaint within 2 working days, investigate it, and give you a full response within 10 working days. If we need longer, we will explain why. A director will review any complaint that is not resolved at the first stage.

Accessibility Statement

We want everyone to be able to use this website. It is designed to work with screen readers and keyboard navigation, with descriptive text for images, good colour contrast, and pages that resize for phones and tablets. Animations stop if your device is set to reduce motion.

If you have difficulty using any part of the website, contact us on 0800 193 9030 or at alex@upsizeproperty.com and we will provide the information in another way.

These statements are approved by the directors of Upsize Property Renovation Ltd and reviewed at least once a year.
